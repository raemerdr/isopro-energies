# Framer templates: direct, services-first, low motion

Checked 26 Oct 2026. Every marketplace listing and live preview was verified to load. Paid templates carry Framer's Single-Use licence (one project). Free ones carry the Limited licence (fine for client work, not for resale).

**German text check (H1 at 390 px mobile):** "Isolierarbeiten" fits in every template below except Northline (2 px short). A 24-letter compound like "Kälteisolierungsarbeiten" overflows the mobile H1 in **all** of them. Keep long compounds out of H1s, or add soft hyphens (`&shy;`) and cap the H1 size.

### Also viable
- **[Induvex](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/induvex/)** ($79, [preview](https://induvex.framer.website/)): has a dedicated Process page and the **smallest H1** (60/36 px), the safest for German. All-caps styling and a more generic look. Services are not CMS.
- **[Manufact](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/manufact/)** (free): has certification, quality-control and quote sections. Aimed at manufacturers, so drop the Product page.
- **[Northline Site](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/northline-site/)** ($69): low motion, a quote form with a division dropdown, Careers CMS. **No Projects collection.** Its huge condensed uppercase H1 is the worst case for German.
- **[Anode](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/anode/)** ($99): the least motion of all, corporate energy/EPC tone. Large H1, no FAQ.

### Rejected
- **Industrial** (fourtwelve, $69): oriented to equipment sales.
- **Factoz:** busy sliders and tickers.
- **Sanayi:** looks like a demo.
- **FORGE, Tilanium, VERTIC:** heavy letter animation or product-catalogue focus.
- **ForgeBuilt:** scroll-linked text gimmick.

## Recommendation
**Use NORDEN Automation as the base.** Its first screen is already the "direct" pattern we used for Treu (claim → one-line lead → primary CTA + secondary link → photo → locations). Its motion is restrained enough that nothing needs ripping out, and it ships with a legal-notice page for the Impressum.

Add to it:
1. A **FAQ** section
2. A **process** section ("Ablauf")
3. A **Karriere** page, borrowed from Northgrid's structure
4. Convert **Solutions to a CMS collection** so each service page shares one layout
5. The signature **pipe cross-section** from `04-interaction-ideas.md`

If the client wants Careers, Safety/certificates and locations from day one and budget isn't an issue, **Northgrid** saves build time. Budget roughly half a day to turn its motion down. **Axil** is the free fallback.
